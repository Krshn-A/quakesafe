What the model is

  • Frame: each floor is a mass and each storey is a spring. The stiffness matrix is tridiagonal.
  • Wall or core: a cantilever beam. Its flexibility matrix comes from the beam deflection formula, and the stiffness matrix is its inverse.
  • Static check: a simplified equivalent-lateral-force procedure in the style of ASCE 7. It uses the computed period directly and ignores the code's cap on that period.
  • Shake test: steady shaking at one frequency with damping proportional to stiffness.
  • Detection: a short-term over long-term average trigger, the P-to-S time gap for distance, and circle equations for the epicentre.

Limits

  • The static check is only valid for regular low and mid-rise buildings. The page warns above about 48 m.
  • The frame model is poor for tall towers. Use the wall or frame + core option, and treat results as a period comparison.
  • No yielding, cracking, twisting or collapse is modelled.
  • One shaking frequency is not an earthquake. Real earthquakes are short and broadband, so real damper benefits are smaller.
  • The damper tuning rule is exact only for a one-storey building with no damping and a force on the main mass.
  • Epicentre: flat map, fixed depth, constant wave speeds (6.0 and 3.5 km/s by default).
  • The damage check fits one overall stiffness factor. It cannot say which storey is damaged.

What is synthetic or assumed

  • Building presets are illustrative, not real buildings.
  • Earthquake load values (SDS, SD1, R, Cd) are examples.
  • Seismograms are generated: noise, a P wave, then a larger S wave.
  • Magnitude uses the Hutton and Boore (1987) Southern California distance correction, and the synthetic amplitude is made from the same formula. It tests the pipeline, not the physics.
  • Stations: UOSS is a real Global Seismographic Network station at the University of Sharjah. The others are hypothetical stations placed at cities.
  • Regression sample rows are synthetic.
  • Slow-shaking bands (1.0–1.75 s and 2.9–4.0 s) are from a summary of a study of the 2005 Qeshm earthquake recorded in the UAE. Check the paper before quoting them.
  • The phone motion-sensor recording has not been tested on a phone.
